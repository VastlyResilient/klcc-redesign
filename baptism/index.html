The next step.

If you have made the decision to follow Jesus Christ, we encourage you to be baptized.

Water Baptism is the next step in your spiritual journey. Prior to a ceremony, we have a preparation class that goes into further detail about what baptism is and its role in your Christian walk. Here you'll explore why you are getting baptized and what it means to you personally.
