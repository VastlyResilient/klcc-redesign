Alpha

A welcoming space to ask big questions about life and faith together.

02

Explore life, faith, and meaning.

We believe everyone should have an opportunity to ask the big questions of life: What is my purpose? What happens when I die? Why is there suffering? Alpha sessions are a connection and conversation spanning the course of 12 weeks that explore those questions and more in a welcoming, judgement free environment. Alpha meets Wednesdays at 7 PM at the Cathedral, with virtual classes available on alternative evenings.
