01 / Plan your visit

New Here

We're so glad you are here! Whether you are new to this church or new to faith, welcome!

Kingdom Life Christian Church is a Christ-centered, Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church which is founded on the belief that God’s Kingdom must be demonstrated “on earth as it is in heaven.” Our congregation draws together people of all races, gender, economic levels, ages, and religious backgrounds. Through Bible-centered preaching and teaching, prayer, practical ministry, and genuine relationships, KLCC encourages and empowers people for the challenges of life. We invite you to join us for a service, a season, or a lifetime.

02 / Plan your visit

Plan a Visit

We want to be sure your first visit is a great experience! We would love to help as much or as little as you’d like, from navigating parking to checking out the campus, or even just introducing your family to some great members of our congregation. We understand that visiting a new church can feel overwhelming, so we’re happy to help lighten the process in a way that’s most comfortable for you.

01

What to Expect

If you're new here, we can’t wait to meet you! We know your time is valuable and we appreciate you using it to worship with us. At Kingdom Life you'll find a diverse community of people with different stories and unique perspectives, all sharing the common truth that we are saved by Jesus Christ. Whether you wear a suit or dress, jeans or shorts, come as you are to experience Jesus with a community who proclaims and demonstrates His love.

02

Arriving at the Cathedral

Upon entering our parking lot we ask that you have your hazard lights on so our parking team can identify you and guide you to our reserved parking section. Once you've parked your vehicle make your way to our main entrance. There our greeters will welcome you and provide you with any information you may need for service. If you have any questions, feel free to ask and they will point you in the right direction.

03

Do you have children?

Children are invited to our Kingdom Kids service! With classes ranging from 6 weeks old to 6th grade, we use interactive media, small groups, and hands-on activities to create a fun, safe environment where they learn that Jesus loves them, and they are valued with an individual and specific purpose! In our Cathedral lobby on the upper level, you can register your child(ren) at the Check-In Hub. Here we securely check in each child to provide you with peace of mind while you enjoy service. Classrooms open 15 minutes before service begins. Once the rooms are open you may drop off your child(ren) with any of our trained volunteers, all of whom have passed background checks.

04

Find Your Seat

As you enter the sanctuary our usher team will be happy to guide you to a seat. As our guest we want you to feel comfortable and invite you to engage with the service however you choose. A typical Sunday morning will consist of singing, greeting one another, and giving. Shortly thereafter a Christ-centered, relevant, practical message will be shared to encourage and challenge you for the week to come.

05

Before You Leave

Please don’t feel that you need to rush out after service—we can’t wait to greet you face to face in our Guest Center located on the right side of our lobby as you are leaving the building. Even if small talk isn’t your thing, just stop by for the free gift. Hope to meet you soon!
