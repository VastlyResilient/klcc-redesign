# Anti-sameness masking

All 30 routes, three viewports, opening and midpoint: 180 frames in nine boards. HTML text rectangles are blurred at 12 pixels in the resized frame; macOS Vision OCR adds detected image-embedded text rectangles. Actual header bounds are neutral-masked because a brand-logo selector missed image wordmarks in initial capture. Known text-bearing campaign/media imagery on Alpha, Calendar, Previous Messages and Media Library is blurred as a whole image to prevent lettering from carrying apparent uniqueness.

This is a composition audit, not anonymization. OCR is fallible: decorative glyphs and tiny image-embedded lettering can remain. Original full-color captures are preserved for legibility and authenticity review. Whole-image masking removes details as well as lettering; that limitation is preferable to claiming unaltered material.

Initial independent inspection of desktop opening/midpoint boards found meaningful variations (full-image Mission, archive tiles, story pairing, portrait gallery, practical contact), but recurring dark left headings and cream right-side reading remain recognizable across several routes. The set is not evidence of universally unique composition or a blanket fidelity pass. Route-specific formal blockers remain in the review records.
