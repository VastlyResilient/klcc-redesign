# KLCC inner-page scroll expansion

The owner requested interactive scrolling throughout the inner website after approving its general direction. Twenty-seven content routes now include bounded responses to native scrolling, alongside their existing chapter sequences. Legal and 404 remain direct utility pages. The homepage baseline is unchanged.

## Verification

- Runtime: 81 route/viewport cases and 291 detail-scene instances, sampled forward, at the midpoint, at the end, in reverse, and with reduced motion. See `runtime.json`.
- Final Pastoral Care anchor check: three cases in `care-final-runtime.json`.
- All 433 content checks and 1,284 link checks preserve existing information and journeys.
- Existing resilience suite: 30 browser routes, 30 no-JavaScript routes, seven interaction journeys, and five WebKit cases.
- Independent visual review and boards are in `independent/`. Raw captures remain local; portable boards show each page at three widths.
- This incremental motion review does not certify the unresolved source-geometry gates. Those remain recorded in `BLOCKED.md`.
