# KLCC inner-page text motion — reference and application record

The homepage film and its existing lettering choreography remain unchanged. This pass adds native-scroll responses to selected **inner-page** reading moments. Every route has at least one target; the route list and selected sections live in `src/motion/text-choreography.ts`. Opening facts, dates, form controls, media players and calls to action stay readable and usable throughout.

Three restrained treatments are assigned by visitor purpose, not by a fixed site-wide pattern: **words** for a short invitation or story heading, **emphasis** for a belief or mission statement, **quiet** for care and explanatory text, and **index** for practical chapter orientation. Most pages receive one or two moments; longer routes receive three. Each target advances from its own viewport position, rewinds on reverse scroll, settles on fast traversal, and restores a fully readable state with reduced motion. No new text is invented and no layout space is removed.

The authored travel and progress ranges are KLCC estimates. Visual QA must check partial, settled and reverse states on desktop and mobile; a captured source screenshot does not certify exact reproduction.
