# Common practices observed in the fresh 21-template pool

Source: `pool-screening.md`, its 21 visual sheets, actual marketplace discovery, and common capture-script DOM records. These are design judgments from viewed captures, not claims of measured easing. Selected sequence dossiers supply deeper temporal evidence.

## Practices to apply inside the single locked world

1. **A subject before decoration.** Royal About, Design & Co projects, Agents About, and Rivelle About let one actual subject occupy most of the opening. A small amount of meaningful type sits in a deliberate relation to it. For KLCC use an actual community/place picture; if none fits, typography leads without a fabricated documentary image.
2. **Practical screens change the composition, not the identity.** Royal Contact keeps the same black, compact uppercase world but uses left contact information and right form instead of a giant image. Lou About changes from full-bleed portrait to an intentionally narrow reading column. KLCC urgent care and logistics should show the answer/action before a theatrical sequence.
3. **A photograph is a section, not an icon.** Royal, Lou, Design & Co and Blissful allocate real screen area to photography. Wide image intervals separate reading work; this is different from adding a small decorative thumbnail beside every paragraph.
4. **Scale contrast comes from restraint elsewhere.** Agents and Lou large words work because the supporting navigation and captions remain small. Royal's compact editorial headlines remain confident through proportion and precise alignment without requiring 120px type everywhere.
5. **Clear asymmetry is stronger than generic cards.** Royal's story text/photo pairing, Slab's metadata relationship, NetFolio's label/body case reading, and Design & Co's text/photo service rows preserve unmistakable reading order. Use real labels and bounded copy instead of equal three-column paragraph boxes.
6. **Ground transitions carry the chapter change.** Lou changes black reading to white footer; Design & Co switches photograph to white details; Perform separates photo, white testimony, black story. Keep KLCC grounds within its locked blue/cream/pale-blue identity, and make each change coincide with a new visitor task.
7. **Archives let their actual objects carry identity.** Slab's dense object gallery and Design & Co's actual project photos read immediately. KLCC series artwork should retain proportions and readable lettering; do not substitute abstract generated covers or invent sermon titles.
8. **Phone composition is a reading rewrite.** Royal contact stacks contact details before form; Slab home becomes one photo column while archive remains a smaller two-column mosaic; Design & Co galleries become a deliberate single-column sequence; Brightedge's sidebar becomes top navigation. Do not merely shrink desktop pinning.
9. **Footer is an intentional destination.** Royal groups pages/contact/newsletter; Lou gives a quiet white contact field and large wordmark; Design & Co makes the wordmark a final strong shape. Preserve KLCC real contact/utility paths without copying a giant footer wordmark everywhere.
10. **Motion must have an observed driver.** The common screening captured fixed headers, property changes and distinct states. It cannot establish every trigger or easing. Only selected deep dossiers authorize reconstructing a sticky interval, word brightening or clip sequence. No generic heading rise as a substitute for an unobserved effect.

## What to avoid from weaker fits

- Atoks pastel metric/card farms and Nouva generic software panels would make KLCC look like a software sales site.
- Brightedge's entire empty ABOUT/CONTACT opening delays practical access; do not recreate that delay for prayer, care or visiting.
- MVN's ornamental floating object/pastiche treatment is not authentic KLCC material.
- Repeating enormous type, floating cards and giant wordmark footers across every route creates another shared formula.
- Template badges, stock faces, invented template metrics and template company claims are not content sources.
- Lou pixelated loading and initial blank loaders found in several templates are technical behavior to avoid, not desired quality.

## Three distinct directions evaluated

1. **Documentary chapters — Royal anchor, Lou reading donor, Slab archive donor (recommended).** Real people and places; compact headings over or above full-width media; story passages retain an adjacent image relationship; expressive reading reserved for mission; archive objects keep their real identity. Best breadth of useful inner-page structures with coherent restrained type. This supports KLCC's practical information and authentic community imagery.
2. **Photographic journal — Rivelle anchor, Royal practical donor, Slab archive donor.** Full-viewport portrait identity, oversized names, bright ruled editorial rows and immediate photographic contact. Strong atmosphere but heavily depends on enough closely coordinated portrait photography; current KLCC assets are less consistent. Do not combine this skin with Direction1.
3. **Community editorial — Agents anchor, Royal practical donor, Slab archive donor.** Oversized tightly tracked words, unequal image grids, confident black ground and project-like ministry stories. Strong youth/program expression but may make care, beliefs and practical notices feel too promotional. Do not combine this skin with Direction1.
