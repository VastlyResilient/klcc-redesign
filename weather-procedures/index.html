01 / Weather Procedures

Stay informed.

Services are held as scheduled unless severe weather conditions exist.

In the event that a service is affected due to weather, we will either delay opening, or hold service online only. Please use discretion when making the decision to travel during poor weather conditions.

02 / Weather Procedures

Tune in to these media outlets

Download our KLCC App and turn on notifications to receive KLCC service changes and updates directly to your smart phone. Follow us on Facebook and Instagram. Watch TV NEWS 12, and WFSB 3.
