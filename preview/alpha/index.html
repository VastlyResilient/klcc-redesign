A place for your questions

The questions

We believe everyone should have an opportunity to ask the big questions of life: What is my purpose? What happens when I die? Why is there suffering?

The conversation

Alpha sessions are a connection and conversation spanning the course of 12 weeks that explore those questions and more in a welcoming, judgement free environment.

When we meet

Alpha meets Wednesdays at 7 PM at the Cathedral, with virtual classes available on alternative evenings.
