Mission and Vision

MISSION.

Proclaim and Demonstrate

Our Mission

To proclaim and demonstrate the gospel of the Kingdom and the rule of Christ as God’s solution to the kingdoms of this world.

Our Vision

  1. Reach people with the love of God and the hope of Jesus.
  2. Teach and train practical biblical principles.
  3. Break down walls that exist in church and culture.
  4. Be a place of restoration physically, emotionally and spiritually.
  5. Prepare the next generations to live in their God-given identity and purpose.
  6. Serve the community with humility and compassion.
  7. Connect with churches and ministries in the spirit of unity.
