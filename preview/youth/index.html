Youth Ministry

ELEVATE

Grades 7–12

Elevate youth ministry is for teens in 7th–12th grades. Every week, we meet in an environment where we can feel encouraged to question, learn, and grow, using various ways to present the Gospel message in a relevant way for today’s culture.

Every Wednesday

7:30 PM

Doors open at 6:45 PM · Youth Center

Youth Service

Elevate services are on Wednesdays at 7:30 PM at the Youth Center. Doors open at 6:45. Come hang out with us! It’s a place to connect, worship, and grow with friends who are all about faith, fun, and real community.
