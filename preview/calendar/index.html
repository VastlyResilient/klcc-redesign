  1. Ongoing series

    Alpha Fall LIVE

    September 23, 2026 · 7:00 PM EDT — December 9, 2026 · 9:00 PM EST

    Event details

  2. Ongoing series

    Discipleship Courses

    September 23, 2026 · 7:00 PM EDT — November 11, 2026 · 9:00 PM EST

    Event details

    Our Wednesday Night Discipleship Courses provide a place for you to learn, connect, and grow at every stage of your walk with God. Learn from practical, Bible-centered classes to build your foundation, explore your faith more deeply, and grow in wisdom for your everyday life. Go deeper! Grow stronger! Take your next step! — COURSE OFFERINGS - Courses run simultaneously on Wednesdays from 7-9 PM. Please select only one. • APOLOGETICS FOR EVERYONE - Why believe in God? Can the Bible be trusted? What do we do with questions about suffering? This course explores some of the biggest questions about Christianity and helps you build confidence in what you believe and why. • EVANGELISM MASTER CLASS - Want to share your faith but not sure where to start? This course will help you grow in confidence and learn how to have natural, authentic gospel conversations through biblical teaching, real-life stories, and practical exercises. • FINANCIAL PEACE UNIVERSITY - Take control of your money and achieve financial peace with Financial Peace University (FPU). This nine-lesson course based on biblical principles provides a step-by-step plan to get out of debt, save for the future, and build wealth while offering a supportive community for accountability and lasting change.
