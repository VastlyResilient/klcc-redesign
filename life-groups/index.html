Life Groups

Life is better together!

12-week trimesters · In person & online

Find your people

Whatever your
stage of life.

Life Groups meet in 12-week trimesters throughout the year where we can engage with one another, build intentional connections, and create friendships and belonging. Groups meet at varying times and in varying demographics, from general (open to anyone) to more specific, like new parents. Whatever your age or stage of life, there is a place for you. To better serve you, we meet using a hybrid model where some groups meet online while others may meet in person.

Find a Group that Best Fits Your Life and Schedule:

general

For young and old, single or married, in college or a parent, any age and stage of life is welcome in our General groups.

women

A women-only group where ladies of any age and stage of life are invited to enjoy sisterhood camaraderie.

men

Become stronger men. Focus on the betterment as individuals, and as the spiritual head of your families and marriages.

young adults

For young adults ages 18-25 to grow and connect with others of similar age and stage of life.

new parents

New to parenthood? Connect with like-minded, first-time parents, glean from valuable insights, and build community during your early years of parenting.

believers

If you’re just beginning your Christian walk or want a refresher in what you believe, this group is for you.

creatives

For creative musicians, vocalists, artists, and more.
