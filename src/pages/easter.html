What to expect

Ask the church a question

When do doors open?

Our doors open at 9:45 AM on Sunday. After you’re greeted by our Hospitality Team, our Usher Team can guide you to your seats.

Can I bring my kids?

Of course! Programming is designed for children from 6 weeks old through 6th grade.

How long is the Easter service?

The Easter service was planned to be approximately 75 minutes long.

Will I be singled out?

Not at all. As our guest, we want you to feel comfortable and engage however you choose. Singing, greeting one another, and giving are ways to participate, but please do not feel obligated if any of those make you uncomfortable.

We’d love to meet you.

Following the close of service, guests were invited to either Guest Reception area in the lobby to meet the team and receive a special gift. If you have a question that isn’t answered here, our team will be happy to help.
