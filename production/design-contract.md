## Palette and typography

The white logo, inky worship venue, vivid blue ceiling and violet floor are the brand cues actually visible in KLCC's source imagery. Use ink `#14172b`, muted indigo `#28385f`, plum-indigo `#20243b`, cool periwinkle `#bfd1ec`, neutral near-white `#f2f3f3` and white `#f8f9fc`. Periwinkle is a narrow emphasis color, not a second competing brand bar. Avoid the previous gold text against electric-blue rail. Archivo Black remains the selected bold chapter/display voice; Barlow supports readable navigation and body text. The headline and rail share the same blue-violet family across desktop and mobile.

A no-JavaScript and reduced-motion path reveals content without requiring the cinematic sequence.
