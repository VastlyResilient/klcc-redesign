02 / ELEVATE YOUTH

Youth Service

Elevate youth ministry is for teens in 7th to 12th grades. Every week we meet in an environment where we can feel encouraged to question, learn, and grow using various mediums for presenting the Gospel message in a relevant way for today’s culture.

WEDNESDAYS / YOUTH CENTER

7:30 PM

Doors open at 6:45 PM.

Elevate services are on Wednesdays at 7:30 PM at the Youth Center. Doors open at 6:45. Come hang out with us! It’s a place to connect, worship, and grow with friends who are all about faith, fun, and real community.
