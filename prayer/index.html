01 / Prayer

The Power of Prayer

Sunday Morning 7-8 Men's Prayer • Cathedral Lobby 8:30-9:30 Corporate Prayer • The Meeting Space — Monday Night 6:30 Men's Prayer • Fellowship Court

We are a church founded on prayer and still today we believe God provides all we need through prayer. Having already experienced how God responds to prayer, as a community and individually, we invite you to join us in prayerful agreement believing that the presence of God will be available to all, on earth as it is in heaven. Join us as we seek God's presence and believe for a touch from Heaven for our church, our families, our city, our region, and our world as we pray together — "come, Holy Spirit."
