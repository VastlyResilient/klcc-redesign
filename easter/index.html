03 / Easter Sunday

What to Expect

Our doors open at 9:45 AM on Sunday. After you're greeted by our welcoming Hospitality Team, you can make your way to your seats guided by our Usher Team. Can I bring my kids? Of course! We offer programming designed specifically for children from 6 weeks old thru 6th grade.

Continue reading

How long is the Easter service? Our Easter service is approximately 75 minutes long. Will I be singled out in any way? Not at all. As our guest, we want you to feel comfortable and invite you to engage with the service however you choose. We offer a variety of ways to interact within the service, singing, greeting one another, giving, but please do not feel obligated to participate if any of those make you feel uncomfortable. We’d love to meet you. Following the close of service, we invite you to stop in the lobby at either of our Guest Reception areas so we can meet you in person and give you a special gift. Have a question that wasn’t answered? Our team will be happy to help answer your questions. Click here to CONTACT US.
