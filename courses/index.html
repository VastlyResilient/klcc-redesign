Wednesday Nights at KLCC are for Discipleship!

Looking for Bible-centered classes to help you grow in your faith and apply God's wisdom to everyday life? Our Wednesday night discipleship courses are a place to learn, connect, and grow in your walk with God. Go deeper. Grow stronger. Take your next step.

Fall Courses

September 23 thru December 9

Alpha 9/23 thru 12/9

Alpha is a place to explore life's big questions: What is my purpose? What happens when I die? Why is there suffering? Over 12 weeks, people come together to share throughs, hear different perspectives, and have honest conversations in a judgment-free environment.

Apologetics 9/23-10/28

Why believe in God? Can the Bible be trusted? What do we do with questions about suffering? This course explores some of the biggest questions about Christianity and helps you build confidence in what you believe and why.

evangelism 9/23-11/11

Want to share your faith but not sure where to start? This course will help you grow in confidence and learn how to have natural, authentic gospel conversations through biblical teaching, real-life stories, and practical exercises.

Finance 9/23-10/28

Take control of your money and achieve financial peace with Financial Peace University (FPU). This 9-week course based on biblical principles provides a step-by-step plan to get out of debt, save for the future, and build wealth while offering a supportive community for accountability and lasting change.

03

Winter Courses

Bible Study

How to Study the Bible is an 8-week, hands-on course that helps you understand and apply Scripture in daily life. You’ll learn how to observe, interpret, and live out God’s Word through engaging videos, group discussions, and weekly homework. Whether you're just starting or want to grow deeper, this class will build your confidence in studying the Bible.

04

Spring Courses

Prayer Course

Explore the meaning and power of the Lord’s Prayer in this 8-week course. Through engaging discussion and uplifting teaching, you’ll grow closer to God and strengthen your faith. Whether you're just starting out or looking to go deeper, discover how this timeless prayer can guide your daily life.

Habits of the household

This course is for parents with children up to middle school age. Find peace in the middle of everyday chaos. Learn simple daily habits and easy-to-implement routines that help you create a home where your whole family can grow closer to God and each other.

05

Other Courses

Marriage

The Marriage Course is a 7-session series designed to help couples strengthen their relationship—whether you've been married for months or decades, and whether things are going well or you're facing challenges. You'll learn practical skills like better communication, resolving conflict, understanding each other’s needs, and building deeper intimacy.
